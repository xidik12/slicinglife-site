MiyZapis

Book a specialist in one conversation

A booking marketplace and business platform for appointment-based services. Customers search for a specialist, or simply describe what they need to an AI concierge that checks live availability and books. Specialists and salons run their whole day on the same system: calendar, clients, loyalty, staff, stock, payroll and accounting.

  • Marketplace with live availability from real calendars
  • AI concierge that searches, checks and books in Ukrainian, English or Russian
  • Loyalty, gift cards and memberships
  • CRM, HR, payroll, inventory and accounting for salons
  • Web app, mobile app and Telegram mini-app
Visit miyzapis.com

Founded by Khidayotullo Salakhitdinov

PharmKulen

Find any medicine at a licensed pharmacy near you

Cambodia's medicine search and pharmacy management platform. Patients type or photograph a medicine, in Khmer or English, and see which licensed pharmacies nearby have it in stock and at what price, then reserve it and pay by KHQR. Pharmacies run inventory, point of sale, expiry alerts and AI sales analytics on PharmKulen, and their live shelf data powers the search.
