About the studio

A small team that makes games and writes the music for them.

The first release was music. Inauguration came out in 2021, and more followed: Samurai Spirit, Macabre Life, Koi No Hajimari and a run of singles, each with its own visuals on YouTube.

Then came the games. Little Wanderers Flights was the first, with a single of the same name. Today the studio has two games in stores, Slicing Life and Springbound, a hero-defense game in beta, and three more in the workshop.

We build everything in house: design, code, art direction, sound and release.
