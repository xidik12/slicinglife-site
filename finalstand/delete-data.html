Delete your account and data

Final Stand: The Last Grove · developer: Incognito Generation

You can delete your Final Stand: The Last Grove account and its data at any time, from inside the game or by email.

Option 1: in the game

  • Open the game and go to Settings → Account.
  • Tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the game (for example you no longer have the device), email xidik12@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include:

  • your nickname and friend tag (for example Name#1234);
  • the platform you played on (iPhone/iPad, Android, Mac or Windows).

We may ask one question to confirm the account is yours, then delete it and reply to confirm. We aim to complete email requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your player profile and progress (heroes, cosmetics and other owned items).
  • Your currency balances (Grove Marks and Amber).
  • Your leaderboard entries.
  • Your friends list, friend requests and blocks.

Chat messages and voice are never stored, so there is nothing to delete for them.

What may be kept

Records of real-money purchases (product, transaction ID and date) may be kept for as long as accounting, tax or fraud-prevention law requires. They are not used for anything else. The app stores (Apple, Google, Valve) keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Before you delete

  • Purchases and currency cannot be restored after deletion.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Warden's Pact subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account first. See Support.
