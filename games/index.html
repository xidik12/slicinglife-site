From one-thumb arcade games to five-player hero defense. Here is everything we have out, in beta and in the workshop.

Final Stand: The Last Grove

Hero defense for one to five players

An ancient tree is the last thing standing between the grove and the dark. Pick a champion, master four skills, build your gear between waves and hold the line against bosses and commanders. Play solo, defend together with friends or face another team in versus.

2-5players in online co-op
5v5team versus, same waves
4solo difficulty tiers

Platforms: iOS, Android, Windows and Mac. Status: in beta testing.

Slicing Life

Cut the shape. Dodge the rest.

Run the edge, dive across the middle and slice off everything you do not need. Time it wrong and the hazards you were dodging take the run instead. A daily challenge puts the whole world on the same level, and endless mode keeps climbing past level 100.

100hand-tuned levels in ten acts
16runners with their own powers
10kinds of hazard to dodge

Platforms: iOS and Android. Price: free.

Springbound

Leap through a luminous endless garden

A one-thumb platformer about timing. Aim your spring, launch, land clean on the next platform and see how far up the garden you can climb before a miss sends you back to the start.

Platforms: iOS now, Android coming. Price: free.

Little Secrets

A hidden-object mystery

Stand in one room at a time, turn, zoom and search. Every room hides its own checklist of evidence among the clutter of someone's life, from a double-height library and an iron conservatory to an observatory dome, a theatre balcony and a train carriage.

120rooms in twelve collections
720hidden finds to uncover
80kinds of object to spot

Platforms: iOS, Android and Mac. Status: in development.

Elemental Arena

Elemental creatures that fight by your voice

Create an elemental beast, then battle it by talking: say your plan, and an AI strategist and narrator call each round out loud. It runs on a palm-sized voice gadget with its own screen, mic and speaker, and in a web browser with no hardware at all.

Elemental Arena won the Robotics and Physical AI track at Agentic AI Build Week 2026 in Ho Chi Minh City.

7elements, from fire to poison
25beast species
1stRobotics and Physical AI track

Platforms: voice puck and web browser. Status: private beta.

Pose Runner

Your body is the controller

A family runner on the big screen, controlled by a phone camera. Run on the spot, jump, duck, punch and lean to dodge obstacles together. Body tracking happens on the phone, so video never leaves it. Scan a code and up to four players are in.

4players at once
5moves read from your body
3modes: solo, co-op, versus

Platforms: TV or laptop browser with a phone as the controller, Android TV. Status: in development.
