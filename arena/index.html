Two lives

One puck. A friend and a fighter.

Say its name and it wakes up: it tells a story, runs a quiz in your language, remembers what you talked about yesterday, or looks at what you're holding. Say "start a battle" and the same device becomes a fighter: its beast appears on screen, an AI strategist plans each round, and it narrates the fight while your opponent's puck, across the table or across the world, narrates the other side.

  • 240x240 screen where the beast lives, microphone and speaker
  • Wake word on device, updates over WiFi, no app needed
  • Messages to friends: "tell Frost I said hi"
  • Camera build that can see what you're holding

Rock, paper, scissors with six hands

Seven elements. One ring.

Each element beats the next one around the circle: fire, ice, air, earth, lightning, water, then fire again. Hit someone you're strong against for x1.75 damage, someone you're weak against for x0.67. Poison is the odd one out: strong against water and earth, weak against fire and air, and nothing is strong against it.

Tap an element to see what it beats.

The Momentum Duel

Plan together. Strike together.

Both players plan at the same time. By voice you just say "start a battle" and the AI strategist plans and narrates every round.

Energy

Every round each creature spends energy on attacks and guards. More energy, harder hit. Swift fighters get more, tough ones get more health.

Guards

A guard softens a hit, never by more than three quarters. At least 1 damage always gets through.

Momentum

Clean hits fill the bar. At 100 your creature fires its Ultimate by itself, for about double damage.

Sudden death

Nobody goes home with a draw. Past the round cap both drop to 6 health and the next clean hit ends it.

Realm, artifacts and ranks

Collect. Merge. Ascend.

Your companion lives in a realm shaped by its element. Fifty-four things to find: wearables that become part of your companion forever when merged, its art redrawn wearing them, and artifacts that turn its land into a home. Ranks climb from C to legendary SSS, and the bonus is deliberately small: buying things never simply buys wins.
