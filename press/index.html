Slicing Life

Cut the shape. Dodge the rest

Download presskit.zip App Store

The pitch

Slicing Life is a pixel-art arcade slicer for one thumb. You run the edge of a shape, dive across the middle to cut a piece off, and keep going until only the target sliver is left — while ten kinds of hazard try to catch your line mid-dive. The campaign is 100 hand-tuned levels across ten acts, taught one hazard and one silhouette at a time; sixteen runners each change how a run plays rather than how it looks; a server-scored Daily Challenge gives everyone the same shape and the same six cuts once a day; and Endless picks up past level 100 for as long as you can hold on. Free, portrait, offline-friendly, no energy timers and no paywalled levels.

Why it is different

  • The Qix lineage, rebuilt for a thumb. Area-claiming is a 1981 idea almost nobody ships any more; this is that loop on a phone, in portrait, with a modern difficulty curve behind it.
  • Sixteen runners with real powers. Not skins. AEGIS starts every run shielded, CRYO freezes the hazards, CHRONO drags them into slow motion, WRAITH walks straight through border patrols.
  • A server-scored daily challenge. One shared puzzle a day — Classic, MASK, one life, six cuts, no pickups — validated server-side, so the daily board is the one board nobody can talk their way onto.
